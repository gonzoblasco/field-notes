Before you record

This tool listens to someone tell their story. Read this aloud with them before you start:

  • The recording stays on this computer. It is not uploaded to any service.
  • The transcript is automatic and can contain errors. They can correct whatever they want.
  • The final text belongs to them. It is their story, not ours.
  • Everything can be deleted at any time.
